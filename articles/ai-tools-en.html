5 AI Tools Worth Your Afternoon. Not for Novelty — To Take Back Your Time.

Save 2-3 hours daily. That's an extra month every year.

July 22, 2026 · PKU Yan · AI Tools

I'm 53 and I learn AI. Not for bragging rights. Because these tools genuinely saved me 2-3 hours every single day. Here are the 5 I use daily.

1. DeepSeek — Your Thinking Partner

Not just a chatbot. I use it as a thinking sparring partner. Before writing an article, I dump my scattered thoughts into DeepSeek and ask: "Organize this, find the logic gaps, tell me what I'm missing." It doesn't write for me — it helps me think clearer.

2. Kimi — Long Document Analysis

When I need to digest a 50-page industry report in 15 minutes, Kimi is my go-to. Upload the PDF, ask targeted questions. It reads the whole thing so you don't have to.

3. Doubao — Daily Assistant

Quick searches, meeting notes, to-do tracking. ByteDance's model. Fast, accurate, deeply integrated with the Chinese content ecosystem. For everyday tasks, this is my default.

4. Canva AI — Visuals Without a Designer

I'm not a designer. Canva's AI tools let me create decent-looking social media cards, presentation slides, and simple graphics in minutes. The magic is in its template intelligence — it adapts to your content automatically.

5. Feishu Docs + AI — My Second Brain

All my notes, plans, and drafts live here. The built-in AI can summarize meeting notes, extract action items, and even draft follow-up emails. Paired with Obsidian for long-form thinking, this combo runs my entire workflow.

How I Stack Them

Morning: DeepSeek for thinking → Kimi for research → Doubao for quick tasks. Afternoon: Canva for visuals → Feishu for organizing. Evening: Obsidian for reflection.

The goal isn't to use all of them. It's to find the 2-3 that plug your specific gaps and make them daily habits.
