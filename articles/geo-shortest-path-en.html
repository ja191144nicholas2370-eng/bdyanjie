GEO vs SEO: What's the Difference?

SEO fights for webpage rankings. GEO fights for what the AI model says about you. How does an LLM decide who to recommend? Three things: has your content been cited by authoritative sources? Is your information consistent across platforms? Is your structure easy for AI to parse?

An entrepreneur friend asked me last week: "Yan, I've written 80+ Zhihu articles, my WeChat account has 20K followers — why does searching my company name on Doubao only return competitors?" I told him to search his industry's core question on Doubao and see what sources the AI cited. He went silent. 80 Zhihu articles — zero citations. His WeChat articles — all buried. A competitor's industry white paper from three years ago was being cited repeatedly.

The brutal truth of the GEO era: how much you write doesn't matter. Whether AI recognizes you — that's what matters.

Three Shortest Paths

I spent one night distilling China's GEO standards, Weimob's DSS methodology, and my 15 years of brand experience into three paths. No fluff. Each one is actionable.

Path 1: Make Your Content AI-Friendly

LLMs don't read like humans. They scan for: did you clearly ask and answer a question? Is your core argument backed by structured data? You don't need to rewrite everything. Just add one module to each article: a 150-word core summary at the top or bottom, using "what-why-how" in three sentences. That's what AI grabs.

Path 2: Become a Source

Maintain the same topic consistently on at least three platforms. AI cross-validates. Get your views cited by other sources. Give yourself a clear content label — not "I do branding" but "I specialize in full-chain brand building from zero to one." The more specific, the easier for AI to tag you. The more accurate the tag, the more stable the recommendation.

Path 3: Track Three Key Metrics

  • AI Top-Mention Rate — On your industry's core questions, is your brand the first one AI mentions?
  • Source Citation Rate — Does AI cite any of your articles?
  • Brand Mention Frequency — How often does your brand name appear in AI answers?

No paid tools needed. Spend 15 minutes each week: open Doubao, Kimi, and Yuanbao. Search 3 core questions for your industry. Track manually.

The Window Is 6-12 Months

90% of brand strategists are still watching from the sidelines. But Weimob — the company that drafted the national GEO standard — is already doing full-chain GEO optimization for brands across six platforms.

I've seen this movie too many times in 15 years. Traditional ads → search engines → feeds → short video. Every channel migration, the first movers pay the lowest cost and reap the highest return. Four-Week Action Plan

  1. Week 1 — Open Doubao, Kimi, Yuanbao. Search 3 core industry questions. See who AI recommends.
  2. Week 2 — Rewrite 3 best articles using "question-answer-conclusion-data" structure. Add 150-word summary to each.
  3. Week 3 — Open an account on at least one new platform. Post the rewritten content.
  4. Week 4 — Re-search Week 1's questions. See if AI's answers have changed. They will have.
GEO isn't about waiting until AI search is fully mainstream. It's about this: do it now, and AI will remember you in its next model update.
