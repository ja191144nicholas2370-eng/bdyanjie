2026: Brand Rules Have Flipped. Not Slowly — Flipped.

One person with AI can match 5-8 people. Most people are still using 2023 methods for 2026 branding.

July 24, 2026 · PKU Yan · GEO Growth

One person who knows how to use AI can match the output of a 5-8 person team. That's not a prediction — it's already happening. And most people are still doing 2026 branding with 2023 methods.

Three Things Happened in One Summer

July: China Advertising Association officially released the Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) standard. GEO went from "industry buzzword" to national-standard infrastructure. First movers eat红利. Latecomers pay premiums.

Almost simultaneously, Xiaohongshu began testing AI search assistant "Ask Diandian." Users no longer search keywords — they ask questions. Whether your content gets cited by AI determines whether you appear in the answer.

WeChat Search is also upgrading its AI search. Users ask "how to do personal IP positioning" — AI gives a direct answer, no link list.

Before: User searches "Yan's IP positioning" → sees your article link → clicks
Now: User asks "how to find my IP positioning" → AI summarizes the answer → if your content is cited, you're in the answer; if not, you don't exist

The Rules of Winning Have Changed

I've done branding for 15 years. Traditional channels → e-commerce → content marketing — every rule change eliminates some and elevates others. But this time is different. Previous changes were "the channel changed." This time "the entry point changed." People don't enter through a link list anymore — they enter through an AI-generated answer.

  • Not about ad spend — it's about whether your content can be cited by AI
  • Not about keyword rankings — it's about whether your content structure matches AI's understanding logic
  • Not about volume of posts — it's about whether your "question→answer" structure is well-built

What is GEO? Let Me Show You

Say you're a brand positioning consultant. Before: write a "how to do brand positioning" article on Zhihu, optimize keywords, wait for search rankings. Now: a user asks AI directly "I'm a 35-year-old professional wanting to transition into personal IP — how do I find my positioning?" If your content has clear Q&A structure with specific scenarios, AI cites you in the answer.

Three actions right now: restructure one article in Q&A format, add structured summaries to your best content, maintain consistent information across platforms.
