SEO vs GEO — What Changed?

Traditional SEO fights for webpage rankings. GEO fights for what the AI model says about you.

When a user asks Doubao "which brand is reliable in this industry," the answer isn't a list of links — it's an AI-generated summary. If your content is in the source list, you exist. If not, you don't.

SEO: You knock on the user's door.
GEO: When a user asks AI "who's trustworthy," AI knocks for you.

Three Things That Happened This Summer

July: China Advertising Association releases GEO standards. GEO moves from "industry buzzword" to national-standard infrastructure.

Same time: Xiaohongshu begins testing AI search assistant "Ask Diandian." Users no longer search keywords — they ask questions. Whether your content appears depends on whether AI can cite it.

WeChat Search is also upgrading AI search. Users ask "how to do personal IP positioning" — AI gives a direct answer, not a link list.
